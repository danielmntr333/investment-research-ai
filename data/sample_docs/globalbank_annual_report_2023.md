## Letter to Shareholders

Dear Shareholders,

2023 was a year of strategic execution and resilience for GlobalBank. Despite a challenging macroeconomic environment marked by rising interest rates and heightened regulatory scrutiny, we delivered strong financial results while strengthening our capital position and advancing our digital transformation.

- Net income of $18.2 billion, up 8% from 2022

As we enter 2024, we remain focused on three strategic priorities: deepening client relationships, accelerating digital innovation, and maintaining operational excellence.
